\chapterhead{3}{A Yes-or-No Key}{Splitting a group with one question at a time}\label{ch3}

A \textbf{key} is a list of yes-or-no questions that sorts a thing into a group. You answer the first question. The answer tells you which question to ask next. When there are no more questions, you have an answer.

Every yes-or-no question splits one group into two smaller groups. Here is a key that sorts all eight bugs, one question at a time.

\begin{worked}{An example}
Bug G has no wings, has spots and has long antennae. Wings? no. Spots? yes. Long antennae? yes. It ends at G. Each bug follows its own path, and no two bugs share one.
\end{worked}

\begin{puzzle}{Fill the gap}
