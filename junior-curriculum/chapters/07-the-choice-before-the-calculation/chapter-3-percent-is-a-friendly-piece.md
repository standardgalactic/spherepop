# The Choice Before the Calculation

## Chapter 3: Percent Is Just a Friendly Piece

A sign in the shop window said **15% off**. The jacket cost 80 dollars. Rina wanted to know how much she would save.

"15 percent of 80," said Rina. "That sounds hard. But I get to choose how to cut it."

### Step 1: Cut the percent into friendly pieces

Some percents are easy. **10%** of a number is that number divided by 10. **5%** is half of 10%. **1%** is the number divided by 100.

15 is 10 plus 5. So 15% of 80 is 10% of 80 plus 5% of 80.

```
( 15% of 80 )
redraw  →  ( ( 10% of 80 ) + ( 5% of 80 ) )
pop  →  ( 8 + 4 )
pop  →  12
```

Rina saves **12 dollars**. The jacket costs 80 − 12 = **68 dollars**.

### Step 2: Different percents, different pieces

Rina could pick pieces that fit the percent she had.

**35% of 60.** Use 30% and 5%. 10% of 60 is 6, so 30% is 18, and 5% is 3.

```
( 35% of 60 )  →  ( 18 + 3 )  →  21
```

**25% of 48.** 25% is a quarter, so divide by 4. 

```
( 25% of 48 )  →  12
```

**99% of 200.** 99 is almost 100. 100% of 200 is 200, and the 1% that is missing is 2.

```
( 99% of 200 )  →  ( 200 − 2 )  →  198
```

Look at the pattern. Rina did not run one method on every problem. She looked at the percent, then chose the friendly piece that fits.

### Step 3: Name the thing you want

One more choice: **what do you want to find?** Sometimes Rina knows the saving and wants the price, and sometimes it is the other way round.

The jacket saves 12 dollars out of 80. To find the price she pays, she can either:

- subtract the saving: 80 − 12 = 68, or
- notice that paying 85% means 10% plus 5% less than the whole: 80 − 8 − 4 = 68.

Both give 68. Pick the one that matches the question. If the sign said "pay 85%", Rina would use the second. If the sign said "save 15%", the first.

### What did we keep?

Write the answers in your notebook.

- **What stayed the same after we cut 15% into 10% and 5%?** (The value, 12.)
- **What changed?** (The work: two easy steps instead of one hard one.)

### What makes this trustworthy?

The reason is that percents add. 10% of a number plus 5% of the same number is 15% of that number, because both are parts of the same whole. So the redraw keeps the value.

The check is to try a different cut. For 15% of 80, use 20% minus 5%: 16 − 4 = 12. ✓ Two different cuts that agree make a slip unlikely.

### Practice

1. Find 15% of 60.

2. Find 20% of 35.

3. Find 45% of 40. (Hint: 40% and 5%.)

4. Find 98% of 50.

5. A bike costs 120 dollars and is 15% off. What does it cost after the discount?

6. Rina finds 15% of 80 as 20% minus 5%. Show her notebook in circles.

### Answers

1. 10% is 6 and 5% is 3, so **9**.
2. 10% is 3.5, so 20% is **7**.
3. 10% of 40 is 4, so 40% is 16, and 5% is 2. 16 + 2 = **18**.
4. 100% of 50 is 50 and 2% is 1, so **49**.
5. 10% is 12 and 5% is 6, so 15% is 18. The price is 120 − 18 = **102 dollars**.
6. ( ( 20% of 80 ) − ( 5% of 80 ) ) → ( 16 − 4 ) → **12**.
